Usage

Rules

The short list. These keep everything looking and sounding like one company.

Logo

  • Give it clear space equal to the height of the "E" on all sides.
  • Keep the full logo at least 120px wide on screen.
  • Green and grey on light backgrounds, green and white on dark backgrounds or photos.
  • The eagle head alone works for icons and watermarks (4–8% opacity).
  • Never stretch it, re-space the letters, add shadows or glows, or put the green version on green.

Color & Layout

  • Alternate backgrounds white, light grey, dark grey down a page.
  • Green is for accents and CTA bands, never a full-page background.
  • Corners stay squared: 2–6px, nothing soft and bubbly.
  • The 3px green underline under a heading is our signature detail.
  • No brand-color gradients, patterns or textures.

Voice

  • "We" for Eagle, "you" for the client. Not "customers" in body copy.
  • Hero headlines in ALL CAPS, section headings in Title Case, body in sentence case.
  • Ampersands over "and" in headings and navigation: Signs & Trade Show Displays.
  • One exclamation point per block, at most: Call us today!
  • Short CTAs: Request Demo, Get Your Demo.
  • No emoji. No "platform", "seamless", "leverage" or "empower".
